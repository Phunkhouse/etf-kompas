01

Product and rules

What the site is for and the rules every screen follows. If a screen and a rule disagree, the rule wins.

What it is

A Czech-language comparison for people who want to buy American stock indexes (S&P 500, Nasdaq 100) from Czechia: which European fund tracks the index, which broker sells it, and what it costs over the years, in koruna.

The hook

„Americké indexy z Česka: u koho je koupíte a za kolik?“ American VOO and QQQ are what people search for, but most Czech brokers will not sell them. The page compares the European twins (CSPX, VUAA, VUSA, CNDX, EQQQ) and asks where and for how much.

Who it is for

Beginners who read in a few seconds. Plain Czech, no jargon (ETF is explained once, in one sentence), native copy that David reviews himself.

What it is not

No advice, no forecasts, no „nejlepší“. Costs and availability only, never returns. Nothing is sold, no commissions.

Rules that hold everywhere

  • Never „nejlevnější“Not without verified prices for every broker. A highlighted row means „buyable from Czechia“, not „cheapest“.
  • Every number has a sourceSource and month next to it. A „Ne“ needs a source per broker. Anything unverified is the amber pill [ověřit], never a guess.
  • One illustrative assumption6 % a year, the same for every fund, always labelled „příklad, ne slib“, shown net of fees. Czech taxes and inflation are not counted and the page says so.
  • The green saving box is a maximum„až X XXX Kč“, „při vašem nastavení“, „Odhad, bez českých daní a inflace“. Shown only when at least two brokers that offer the chosen fund have verified prices; otherwise the box and its button disappear.
  • CurrencyEach broker is priced at its cheapest listing of the fund (rev. 6), so the row reads „převod korun na cizí měnu“ (it was „na dolary“).
  • Disclaimers stay visibleTop bar „Čísla ze stránek fondů a brokerů · ověřeno k 6. 2026“ (the oldest date in the data, rev. 6) and „Investování nese riziko ztráty“, plus the footer text on every page. Not investment or tax advice.
  • ConsentThe comparison needs only an e-mail. The tips checkbox is optional, unticked, and confirmed by a second e-mail (double opt-in). Measurement and advertising cookies stay off until the visitor agrees.
  • Placeholders look like placeholdersX XXX Kč for computed amounts, [ověřit] for unverified facts, [doplnit] for legal gaps, [SPRÁVCE] for the controller.

Hero variants for the A/B test

  1. What is testedHeadline framing: a loss-framed number against the neutral question (SPEC section 11, hypothesis 2). Metric: visit to calc_start. Guard metrics: bounce and lead quality.
  2. Variant a (?v=a, default)H1 „Americké indexy z Česka: u koho je koupíte a za kolik?“. This is the landing page drawn above.
  3. Variant b (?v=b)H1 „U stejného fondu můžete zbytečně zaplatit až X XXX Kč. Kde?“ Everything else is identical: layout, lead, table, calculator, form, buttons. The non-breaking spaces keep „X XXX Kč“ on one line.
  4. Honest numberThe amount is the saving box maximum for the default setting (3 000 Kč a month, 10 years). One line under the table says so: „Částka v nadpisu: 3 000 Kč měsíčně, 10 let, odhad bez českých daní a inflace.“ Variant b is served only when the saving box hide rule is met (two brokers with verified prices). Otherwise every visitor gets a.
  5. AdsBoth ad sets send traffic to both variants, split equally. Ad angle and headline are separate factors.
  6. Not tested hereCalculator-first against table-first (dropped), gate placement (hypothesis 1) and form friction (hypothesis 3). They run later as separate tests, in that order of expected impact.
  7. Before launchCheck that the hero still fits 375 × 667 with the longer headline and the extra note (SPEC section 22). track() and the variant field on every event must exist, or the test cannot be read.
05

Calculator

It answers one question: what does the fund you can actually buy cost over the years, with the broker as the lever. It ends on something positive, but only when the data supports it.

  1. Question„Kolik vás to bude stát?“
  2. InputsMonthly amount 500 to 20 000 Kč in steps of 500, default 3 000 Kč, shown right-aligned and large. Period chips 5 / 10 / 20 years, default 10. One investment a month.
  3. Result„Za 10 let vás fond (CSPX) může u ověřených brokerů stát i 30 778 Kč“ (rev. 6; the amount is the real calculator result at the dearest verified broker), then the three parts: roční poplatek fondu, převod korun na cizí měnu, poplatky brokera. Then „Počítáme s výnosem 6 % ročně. Je to příklad, ne slib. Nepočítáme české daně ani inflaci.“
  4. Illustrative line„Ilustrativní hodnota po 10 letech: X XXX Kč, z toho poplatky X XXX Kč · 6 % ročně, příklad, ne slib.“ Net of fees, same assumption for every fund, small, under the result.
  5. Green saving box„Při vašem nastavení · Správným brokerem si u stejného fondu ponecháte až X XXX Kč · Odhad, bez českých daní a inflace.“ Button „Ukázat, u kterého brokera to koupím“, which goes to the e-mail form. The amount is the spread between brokers that offer the chosen fund.
  6. Hide ruleThe box and its button show only when at least two brokers have verified prices. Otherwise the calculator ends with the illustrative line and the form follows. Never „až 0 Kč“ and never an invented number.
  7. Loading and failureLoading: grey placeholders and „Načítáme aktuální poplatky…“, sliders still work. Failure: no amount at all, „Částku teď nespočítáme, aby nebyla nepřesná“, button „Zkusit znovu“.
  8. Why not „expected return“A return figure looks like a forecast and is identical for every fund, so it cannot be part of a comparison. The page shows what the visitor keeps, not what the market might give.
  9. Carry-throughThe visitor's amount and period are carried to the comparison page, the PDF and the e-mail.

Loading and failure

Counted

  • Fund fee, currency conversion, broker fees.
  • 6 % a year, one investment a month.

Not counted

  • Czech taxes.
  • Inflation.
  • Returns as a forecast.

Decide before building

  • Formula and rounding.
  • Which broker prices feed the spread, and what happens with [ověřit] cells (left out, with „mezi ověřenými brokery“).
  • How the setup reaches the e-mail.

10

Motion

Where the page moves, how long it takes and why. The artboard below is live: every demo loops on its own, and the button turns motion off to show the reduced-motion result.

Rules

  1. PrincipleMotion explains a change; it never decorates. Only the hero entrance runs longer than 300 ms, and it plays once.
  2. Timing100 ms press feedback · 150 ms small state (chip, switch, border) · 250 ms panels, boxes, number swap · 500 ms scroll reveal · 700 ms hero entrance.
  3. Easingcubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) for things that appear or move, ease for colour changes. Only opacity and transform are animated, plus panel height.
  4. NumbersThe calculator number swaps (old rises out, new rises in, 250 ms). It is never counted up, and digits are tabular so the width does not jump.
  5. Hide rule and motionThe green saving box grows in under the result only when the hide rule allows it, and leaves the same way. Loading uses a grey shimmer; a failure has no animation, only the message.
  6. Reduced motionWith the system setting „reduce motion“ every animation stops and each screen shows its final state. Meaning never depends on motion: errors are also text, the menu state is also announced.
  7. Not allowedHover movement, looping decoration, parallax, auto-playing carousels, counting numbers.

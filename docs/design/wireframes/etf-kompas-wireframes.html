02

Overview

ETF Kompas trades an e-mail address for an 8-fund ETF comparison. The wireframes cover the whole path from ad to results page.

Core claim

US-domiciled and European-domiciled funds that follow the same big companies differ in cost and in where a Czech investor can buy them. The page shows that difference in crowns for the visitor's own monthly amount.

Conversion goal

E-mail capture on the landing page, plus an optional checkbox for investing tips confirmed with a second click in an e-mail.

Tone

Plain Czech, no advice, no commissions, and every number carries a source and a month („10. 2026“).

Fidelity

Wireframes. Every figure is a placeholder (0,0X %, X XXX Kč, [ověřit]) and must be replaced with a verified, dated value before anything ships.

User flow

  1. Ad A / Ad Bfeed 4:5 · stories 9:16
  2. Landing pagedesktop or mobile; ad A has its own first screen
  3. Calculator → e-mail gateone field, optional tips checkbox
  4. Success screenkeeps the result; opens comparison or 6-page PDF
  5. Comparison page8 funds, Czech taxes, where to buy, method

Hero animation

Only the hero table animates. Reduced motion switches every animation off. T1 is a slower study of the same idea — press the replay button.

  1. Loop12s, review only. Card in 0–6 %, row 1 at 8–16 % (≈1 s), row 2 at 16–24 % (≈1.9 s), row 3 at 24–32 % (≈2.9 s).
  2. Winner highlightFades to --win at 38–48 %, held until 88 %, everything fades out 92–100 %.
  3. ReplayOutline button „Přehrát znovu“, 44px, 14px label. The only scripted behaviour in the whole canvas.
  4. Conflict with MainTimings differ from the production animation in Main (0.4 / 0.9 / 1.4 s, highlight at 2.2 s). Choose one set; in production play once.

Behaviour

The wireframes fix the calculator's inputs, defaults and assumptions but not its formula. Specified means it is in the wireframes; Recommended is a suggestion.

Calculator · specified

  • Monthly amount 500 to 20 000 Kč in steps of 500, default 3 000 Kč.
  • Period 5, 10 or 20 years, default 10.
  • Output „Za {N} let u amerického fondu zaplatíte víc o“ and one amount in three parts: annual fund fee, conversion from crowns to dollars, tax withheld by the USA.
  • Assumption 6 % return per year for both funds, „příklad, ne slib“.
  • Counted: fund fee, currency conversion, foreign withholding tax. Not counted: broker fees, inflation, Czech taxes.
  • Result carried to success and results screens as „3 000 Kč měsíčně · 10 let“.

Calculator · decide before building

  • The formula and the rounding.
  • Which fund pair drives the number.
  • Source and date of each input: fees, conversion cost, withholding rate, dividend yield.
  • Whether the result updates live while the slider moves.
  • What the headline says if the European fund is costlier. „Zaplatíte víc“ assumes the US fund is dearer.

Recommended

  • One pure function with unit tests; every rate from a dated data file; reuse on the methodology page.
  • aria-live="polite" on the result.
  • Intl.NumberFormat('cs-CZ'): space as thousands separator, decimal comma, non-breaking space before „Kč“ and „%“.

E-mail flow · specified

  1. Visitor enters an e-mail and may tick „Chci občas dostat tip k investování (nepovinné)“.
  2. Success screen appears and keeps the calculator result.
  3. An e-mail carries the link to the comparison page, valid 30 days, resendable from the results page. A 6-page PDF is also offered.
  4. Tips need a second click in an e-mail; the comparison does not.
  5. Every e-mail has an unsubscribe link; results footer has „Odhlásit odběr“.

Not specified: validation rules and messages, error and loading states, expired links, whether results work without the link, analytics events, consent logging.

Responsive

Two widths are designed: 1280px and 390px (375px for G5). No breakpoint is given; pick one and test everything between 320px and 1440px. On phones grids collapse to one column, the hero table keeps three columns with shorter labels, results tables become cards with a „show more“ button, the results ToC becomes a scrolling chip row, navigation collapses to „Menu“, and the sticky CTA bar appears on the landing page only.

Open items

What the wireframes leave out, what has to be substantiated, and what to fix in the build.

Referenced but not designed

  • Methodology page („Otevřít postup“, nav item „Jak počítáme“).
  • Privacy policy, cookies page and a cookie consent banner.
  • Unsubscribe page („Odhlásit odběr“).
  • The 6-page PDF itself — pages 1 („srovnání“) and 4 („daně“) appear only as striped placeholders.
  • E-mails: the comparison link, the double opt-in confirmation and „send link again“.
  • The mobile menu behind the „Menu“ chip.
  • A tax page and a where-to-buy page — the success screen links to them while the results page already holds both as sections 2 and 3.
  • FAQ answers — all four questions are shown collapsed.
  • Error, loading and empty states for the form and the calculator.
  • Hover and focus states — only T1-radky defines a row hover (#F3EEDF).

Claims to substantiate before publishing

  • „Oba sledují stejné velké firmy“ and „Stejné firmy. Jiná cena.“ hold only for matched pairs. The results table also lists a world fund, a dividend fund and a technology fund.
  • „Nedostáváme provize“ and „Nic neprodáváme“ must stay true for every link, including broker links.
  • „Zdarma“ and „bez registrace“ stay true only if the calculator result appears before any e-mail request.
  • „Čísla z oficiálních stránek fondů a Finanční správy“ means every figure really comes from those sources; the phone bar drops „a Finanční správy“.
  • The sentences about dollar conversion and tax withheld by the USA need a source per statement.

The page covers investment products in the Czech Republic: have the final copy and the consent flow reviewed by someone qualified before launch.

Settle these first

  1. The calculator formula and the fund pair behind it.
  2. The real European funds (names, ISINs) and the five brokers or apps.
  3. One set of animation timings (Main or T1-radky).
  4. Whether tax and where-to-buy are separate pages or sections of the results page.
  5. Ad export sizes, and the call to action for ad B.
  6. The breakpoint value and the design of the phone menu.
  7. Data controller details and the e-mail provider.

Suggested build order

  1. Foundation — project, tokens, fonts, reset, Logo component (light and dark).
  2. Primitives — Tag, Button, Chip group, Pill, Card, Disclosure bar, Header, Footer, Placeholder image.
  3. Data and calculator — typed data files ({ value, source, asOf }), tested pure function, methodology page.
  4. Landing page — hero, calculator band, gate, cost cards, e-mail preview, trust, FAQ with details, sticky CTA.
  5. Success and results — link token, success screen, results page, resend, PDF.
  6. Ads and polish — render both ads from tokens, work through the accessibility table, send copy for review.

A screen is done when it matches this document at both widths, uses no hard-coded colours, shows no unsourced number, works by keyboard and respects reduced motion.
